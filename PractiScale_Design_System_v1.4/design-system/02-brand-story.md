## The story, at two lengths

Working templates / preserve the voice and verify every factual statement.

### Medium form

Use on the website story section, LinkedIn About, and recruiting decks.

PractiScale started in [year] with one bet: skill can be taught and proven faster than a degree can be earned. We train ambitious people in Pakistan in skills the market pays for, then put them on real US healthcare accounts: sales, virtual assistance, creative and tech. We measure outcomes, not hours, and we publish the proof. If you are hungry and coachable, there is a seat for you. Built for your growth.

### Short form

Use in social bios, job-board blurbs, and email signatures.

We train ambitious people in Pakistan and put them to work on real US healthcare accounts. Hunger over pedigree. Built for your growth.

### Storytelling rules

Tell it through people. Each campaign features a real team member's before and after, in their words, with consent.

Keep the founder a supporting character. The hero of the story is the person reading it.

Never frame Pakistan as a cost-saving location. Frame it as a talent source the world underrates.

Never claim things we cannot show: "best", "largest", "guaranteed income".

Numbers in the story carry a date. Specify the exact count and as-of date, rather than an unsupported "+ and growing" claim.

No company history, team size, pay figure, testimonial, or performance result is established by this template. An internal evidence owner must approve each published claim.
