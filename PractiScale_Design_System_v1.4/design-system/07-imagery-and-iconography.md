# Imagery and iconography

**Fixed principles:** authentic people, consent, privacy, clear overlays, consistent icons. **Open:** final crops, image placement, visual compositions, and website-specific video treatment. No rendered glass-module graphic system is required.

## Photography standards

Credibility comes from authentic detail.

### Light and grade

Use natural or practical light. Evening interiors and screen glow can show the US-hours reality. Keep true skin tones and clean highlights. Shadows may lean gently toward Deep Teal. No heavy teal-orange grade.

### Framing and subject

Eye level, close to mid shots, shallow depth of field. Leave calm negative space for type. Show people doing, not posing: calls, whiteboards, training, reviewing numbers, or celebrating real work. One clear subject per frame.

### Representation

Show the actual range of sales, virtual assistance, creative, and technology roles. Clothing and representation follow each person's own choice.

### Consent and privacy

Keep written consent from each identifiable person, with the right to withdraw. Name and role belong in the caption when the person is the subject. Blur client names and patient data on every visible screen; review each shot.

### Never stage credibility

No stock photographs presented as staff. No AI-generated employees. No staged handshakes, blank-screen pointing, beach laptops, or imagery suggesting easy money.

### Authentic cinematic atmosphere

Future footage may feature the Founder & CEO, the real building, and the working environment. Keep the guidance concise: calm composition, readable live overlays, captions where needed, sound off by default, and a static alternative with identical essential content for reduced motion.

## Icons and moving image

A consistent line system, with accessible alternatives.

### Icon system

Use Lucide, under its ISC license. Use a 24px grid and 1.5px stroke at larger sizes; retain 2px at 16px. Rounded caps and joins remain as supplied. Do not mix icon sets.

### Sizes and color

16px inline; 20px buttons and lists; 24px navigation; 32 to 48px feature tiles. Use text or muted text; accent-text is for active states only. One color per icon. Never rely on color alone.

### Platform assets

Use each platform's official brand resources for platform glyphs. Use a standard envelope icon for email. Do not invent a WhatsApp glyph. No emoji in UI, headings, or job titles; one or two may appear in conversational replies or social captions.

### General video guidance

Start with a person or meaningful action. Caption speech and provide accessible alternatives. Social video may use burned-in captions; website controls and essential copy stay live. Keep logo clear space and readability in any watermark. Use 9:16, 4:5, or 16:9 as the destination requires; verify current platform specifications before export.

## Diagrams and media

Use restrained flat diagrams only when they explain information. Their geometry and placement are not mandatory identity assets. Do not redraw the logo as a diagram. Future footage may feature the Founder & CEO, real building, and actual workplace; a detailed production chapter, storyboard, encoding budget, and scroll sequence are not approved here. Essential content must also work without motion. Inspect any watermark at actual size rather than applying a fixed opacity or percentage blindly.
