# Logo

**Status: fixed artwork and protection rules.** Logo position within a final layout remains open.

## Anatomy and files

The supplied artwork contains the interlocking X, three speed lines, original teal-to-blue gradient, upward-arrow A, and BUILT FOR GROWTH descriptor. Never retype the wordmark or reconstruct a vector approximation as official artwork.

Use the original supplied artwork. Obtain the designer's vector master and approved derivatives before larger production. Do not invent a no-descriptor, stacked, or monochrome version.

The darker blue part of the mark has less separation on Deep Teal and Harbor. Light grounds show the complete symbol more clearly. Inspect the symbol and descriptor at actual size; never recolor them to compensate.

## Clear space and minimum size

Let X equal the cap height of the wordmark, approximately 7% of the full logo width.

### Relative spacing

Keep at least 1X clear on every side of the full logo. Around the mark alone, keep clear space equal to half the mark's height. On spacious layouts, allow 2X to 3X. Never replace this proportional rule with a fixed pixel value.

### Full logo with descriptor

Digital: at least 240px wide.
Print: at least 50mm wide.
Below 240px, use the mark alone. Use a no-descriptor version only after the original designer supplies an approved master.

### Mark alone

Digital: at least 24px.
Print: at least 8mm.
For platform avatars, center with 20% padding; the platform supplies the circular crop.

### Asset limitation

The full logo is 363 x 67px; the mark is 64 x 60px. Raster reuse preserves the original identity, but does not create a print-quality master. Request SVG, AI, or original vector PDF from the logo designer before large-format production.

## Protect the original artwork

Misuse rules apply at every scale.

### Never alter the form

Never stretch, squash, skew, rotate, or set the logo on a path. Never rearrange the mark and wordmark or change their size ratio. Never remove the upward-arrow A. Never retype the wordmark, including in Sora.

### Never add effects

No shadows, glows, outlines, bevels, textures, or recoloring. Keep the interlocking X, three speed lines, original teal-to-blue gradient, wordmark, and descriptor intact. Do not animate the parts separately.

### Never create a sub-brand

Do not combine the logo with another word to create "PractiScale Careers" or "PractiScale PK". Do not enclose it in a badge or container shape, except automatic avatar crops.

### Approved backgrounds

Use primary black-wordmark artwork on Fog, White, or approved light surfaces; reversed artwork on Deep Teal or approved dark surfaces. The dark blue part of the symbol separates more clearly on light grounds. Assess the whole mark at actual size; never recolor it to compensate.

### Avoid conflicting grounds

Never place the logo on Growth Teal, Proof Amber, the primary CTA gradient, or a busy photograph. On imagery, use a calm protected area and verify wordmark readability; a separate solid panel is preferred.

### Co-branding

Client logos belong only in permitted practiscale.com case studies, with written permission: same optical wordmark height, a 1px divider, and 2X space. Official WhatsApp artwork must remain unmodified, smaller than our logo, and secondary in prominence.
