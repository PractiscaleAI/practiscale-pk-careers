# Voice and tone

**Status: fixed voice and writing rules.** Examples are templates. Verify every role-specific claim before publication.

## Four traits

Direct, empowering, transparent, precise.

| We are | Never | Sounds like | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direct | Cold | "The role pays [verified base] plus [verified commission]." | "A highly competitive remuneration package." |
| Empowering | Hype | "You will join a live account after the verified training period." | "Unlock your limitless potential!" |
| Transparent | Oversharing | "Shifts follow US hours. Here are the exact times." | "Flexible timings as per business needs." |
| Precise | Jargon | "Book [verified target] qualified calls a week." | "Drive top-of-funnel synergies." |

Bracketed details are required inputs, not claims. The examples preserve the original voice without publishing unverified pay, timelines, or targets.

## Tone by situation

Same voice, different tone. The reader's state decides.

| Situation / state | Tone shift | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website hero / curious, skeptical | Confident, short | Built for your growth. |
| Open role / hopeful, comparing | Specific, factual | State seat title, verified base, commission terms, and exact US-hours shift. |
| Application received / anxious | Reassuring, concrete | Got it. We will reply on WhatsApp within [confirmed response time]. |
| Assessment / nervous | Clear, supportive | This takes about [verified duration]. There is no trick question. |
| Rejection / disappointed | Respectful, useful | Not this time. Here is the one thing that would change our answer. |
| Offer / excited | Warm, exact | Welcome. Your seat, your pay, and your start date are below. |
| Social / scrolling | Bold, one idea | No degree on the wall. A deal on the board. |
| Client-facing / evaluating | Formal-leaning, results | A trained outreach team, reporting weekly. |

Examples require role-specific confirmation before publication. Never imply a service level, income, or training guarantee that the company cannot support.

## Words we choose

Plain words make the promise easier to understand.

| Use | Avoid | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| seat, role | vacancy, position | A seat is something you take and keep. |
| team | family | We are colleagues. "Family" excuses bad practice. |
| pay, base, commission | remuneration, package, CTC | Plain words. |
| train, coach | upskill, empower as a verb | Say what actually happens. |
| real US accounts | international exposure | Specific beats vague. |
| results, output | hustle, grind | We value outcomes, not exhaustion. |
| apply | submit your candidature | Plain words. |
| fresher | fresh graduate only | Freshers include people with no degree. |
| you, everyone | guys, candidates in headlines | Speak to one person, inclusively. |

Banned: rockstar, ninja, guru, synergy, world-class, limitless, guaranteed income, work hard play hard.

## Copy mechanics

Consistency makes details feel credible.

### Language and case

Use US English spelling: program, color, organize. Use sentence case for headings, buttons, and labels. Uppercase is reserved for overlines.

### Punctuation

No exclamation marks on the site. Use the Oxford comma. Use a clear dash for ranges; avoid ambiguous time or pay ranges.

### Numbers and currency

Use numerals in UI and commas for thousands. Avoid lakh/crore notation outside Roman Urdu social posts. Write PKR [amount] in formal and on-site copy; Rs [amount] is acceptable in Roman Urdu social posts. Always state the pay period and commission conditions.

### Times and dates

State the zone every time. Pakistan time first, US time in brackets. Verify the actual date and US daylight-saving status before publishing a conversion. Use 16 October 2026 in prose and 16 Oct 2026 in UI.

### Names

Write PractiScale as one word, with capital P and S. The all-caps wordmark is artwork, not a spelling.

### WhatsApp

Capital W and A; same font and size as surrounding text. Use it as a noun: Apply on WhatsApp. Do not imply that WhatsApp endorses PractiScale. Use only the official platform glyph.

## Language and action

One verb, one destination, sentence case.

### Languages

English: default for the website, documents, and LinkedIn.

Roman Urdu: conversational Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp replies, and short video captions. Never website headlines.

Urdu script: local campaigns, office signage, and video titles. Use Noto Nastaliq Urdu, right-to-left. Every line needs a native-speaker check before publishing.

### CTA vocabulary

Apply on WhatsApp: primary site CTA.
See open seats: secondary action or navigation label; placement follows the selected layout.
Email your CV: secondary email route.
Start the task: assessment step.
Book a call: practiscale.com only.
Never: Click here, Learn more, Submit, Join the family.

### Rewrite for specificity

"Dynamic go-getters" becomes a clear seat title, training terms, and real account context.

"Competitive salary + perks" becomes a verified base, commission terms, and pay period.

"Freshers can also apply!!!" becomes "Freshers welcome. We will train you." Use this only for roles that include training.

"Interested candidates can WhatsApp at..." becomes "Apply on WhatsApp" with a confirmed destination.

### Do not fill gaps with claims

A pay figure, training duration, hiring target, reply time, or availability statement needs approval from the team responsible for delivering it.
