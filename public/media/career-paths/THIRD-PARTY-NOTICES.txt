PractiScale career section: third-party brain surface

Derived particle positions and normals originate from BrainMesh_ICBM152.mz3,
distributed by Surf Ice (Chris Rorden), with the BrainNet Viewer reference:
Xia M, Wang J, He Y (2013). BrainNet Viewer: A Network Visualization Tool for
Human Brain Connectomics. PLoS ONE 8: e68910.
https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0068910
https://github.com/neurolabusc/surf-ice/tree/master/BrainNet

centering, uniform scaling, rigid orientation and brand-coloured particles.
The source mesh is retained in scripts/assets/career-brain/.
