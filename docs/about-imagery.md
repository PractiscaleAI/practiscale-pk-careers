# About page imagery

The office artwork on the three About pages is art directed from real PractiScale office video frames. It recreates the reception architecture and workspace details while correcting motion softness, uneven exposure, visual clutter, and crops that did not work across screen sizes. Generated people in the workspace artwork are illustrative and must not be identified as actual staff.

The artwork is supplied as optimized WebP. The hero uses a `<picture>` element so tablet and phone widths receive the narrower composition without downloading the desktop source. Keep these scenes described as recreations in alt text and captions. Use the original video footage when a documentary record of the office or team is required.
